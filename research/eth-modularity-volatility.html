Ethereum Modularity

Exploratory links between on-chain community structure and equity volatility events.

Research question

Is Ethereum transaction-network modularity associated with unusually large SPY returns?

Work completed

Implemented hourly weighted graphs, Louvain modularity, UTC panel assembly, event studies, regressions with HAC standard errors, and alternative thresholds and graph weights.

Data and design

Google BigQuery Ethereum transactions, with SPY observations through Yahoo Finance adapters. The configured main sample is 2024, with a separate COVID case study.

Recorded findings

The original project reports falling modularity around equity volatility events, opposite to its initial hypothesis. The archived lag-one ROC figure displays AUC=0.618.

How to interpret the evidence

The current code evaluates ROC on the fitted sample. That AUC is an in-sample fit statistic. Source panels and numerical tables are absent, and network association does not identify a causal mechanism.
