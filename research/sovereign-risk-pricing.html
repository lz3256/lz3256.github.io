Sovereign Risk Pricing

A framework for comparing sovereign market pricing with macroeconomic risk measures.

Risk repricingMethod overview
Repository ↗

Research question

Do differences between market pricing and fundamental risk measures add predictive information beyond existing market signals?

Work completed

Implemented relative cross-country comparisons, expanding-window ridge models, training-only transformations, unfinished-label exclusion, common-sample benchmarks, and sensitivity checks.

Data and research design

The empirical implementation uses JPMaQS via DataQuery; CDS and dollar-bond outcomes are evaluated separately. The public repository contains code, interfaces, and a synthetic demonstration. Source observations, processed panels, and detailed empirical outputs are excluded.

Recorded findings

The recorded primary comparison did not establish stable forecast improvement beyond market baselines. Primary simultaneous intervals included zero.

How to interpret the evidence

A pricing gap is a research hypothesis rather than an established trading signal. Historical versions, model construction, and bond duration exposure affect its interpretation. The README summarizes recorded exploratory findings; withheld empirical inputs and outputs are not included for independent reproduction.
