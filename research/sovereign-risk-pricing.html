Sovereign Risk Pricing

A framework for comparing sovereign market pricing with macroeconomic risk measures.

Risk repricingMethod overview

Research question

Do differences between market pricing and fundamental risk measures add predictive information beyond existing market signals?

Work completed

Implemented relative cross-country comparisons, expanding-window ridge models, training-only transformations, unfinished-label exclusion, common-sample benchmarks, and sensitivity checks.

Research design

The framework evaluates CDS and bond outcomes separately, compares models on matched samples, and distinguishes historical time ordering from independently verified point-in-time data.

Scope of this overview

This overview describes the research design and evaluation framework. Source data, empirical estimates, screening outputs, and performance metrics are not published here.

How to interpret the evidence

A pricing gap is a research hypothesis rather than an established trading signal. Historical versions, model construction, and bond duration exposure affect its interpretation.
