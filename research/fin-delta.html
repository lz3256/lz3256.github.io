FinDelta

Financial numerical reasoning with audited labels and evidence-bound arithmetic.

Financial reasoningLLM post-training
Repository ↗Research protocol ↗Three-seed report ↗

Research question

How do training duration, arithmetic execution, and label quality affect numerical reasoning over financial reports?

Work completed

Built a FinQA data pipeline, LoRA SFT and DPO experiments, an arithmetic executor that checks operands against evidence, and matched label-repair comparisons across three seeds.

Data and design

Custom company-separated FinQA cohort; 1,000 original training questions and 200 development questions. The reviewed training cohort retains 966 questions. Experiments use Qwen3-0.6B.

Recorded findings

Longer SFT improved development accuracy with the same executor. Small-scale DPO did not confirm a gain, and the three-seed label-repair difference remained small.

How to interpret the evidence

Results use a custom development cohort, not official FinQA leaderboard scores. The final test set has not been evaluated, and executor gains must be distinguished from training gains.
