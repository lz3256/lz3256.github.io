Research question

How can a classifier over recent open-price returns connect to a paper-trading workflow?

Work completed

Implemented stacked LSTMs, chronological data splitting, model persistence, connection checks, and an Alpaca paper-trading adapter.

Data and design

1,589 AAPL 30-minute bars from January 23 through July 21, 2025, with OHLC, volume, trade count, and VWAP. Training inputs use ten open-price returns.

Recorded findings

The repository demonstrates the components of the workflow. Its existing backtest is a prototype diagnostic rather than validated strategy performance.

How to interpret the evidence

Target/P&L timing, separately adapted label thresholds, and inconsistent backtest windows require correction. Costs and complete position accounting are not included.
