Research question

Do implied-volatility features add information beyond price-history and HAR benchmarks?

Work completed

Built a shared surface, price, and calendar pipeline; interpolation and node-quality checks; dividend-aware labels; rolling forecasts; and exploratory residualized-skew extensions.

Data and design

optionsDX SPX daily option chains for 2010–2023 and CRSP SPY data. Vendor raw records and processed market inputs are excluded; a separate synthetic demo is available.

Recorded findings

The initial specified one-day return comparison improved holdout MSE by 0.087% with p=0.590. IV levels supplied variance-forecasting information in the recorded sample.

How to interpret the evidence

Return evidence is weak. Stronger variance significance depends on the multiple-comparison family. Later extensions reuse the original sample and former holdout, so their findings are exploratory.
