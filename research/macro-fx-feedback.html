Macro Surprise Decay and FX Feedback

A framework for studying the persistence of macro surprises and feedback between FX and financing conditions.

Macro informationMethod overview

Research question

Does the predictive relationship between macro surprises and FX returns decay, and how does it vary with economic conditions?

Work completed

Developed a framework with comparable holding windows, lagged state controls, synchronized block resampling, expanding-window prediction, and time-alignment audits.

Research design

The design distinguishes information dates from economic reference periods, uses common event samples across delay comparisons, and separates descriptive relationships from out-of-sample prediction.

Scope of this overview

This overview presents the research questions and implemented methods. It does not publish source datasets, empirical charts, coefficients, or performance metrics.

How to interpret the evidence

Predictive feedback does not establish a causal loop. Interpretation depends on data vintages, observation timing, sample coverage, and the family of comparisons.
