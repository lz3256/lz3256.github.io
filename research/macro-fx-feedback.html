Macro Surprise Decay and FX Feedback

A framework for studying the persistence of macro surprises and feedback between FX and financing conditions.

Macro informationMethod overview
Repository ↗

Research question

Does the predictive relationship between macro surprises and FX returns decay, and how does it vary with economic conditions?

Work completed

Developed a framework with comparable holding windows, lagged state controls, synchronized block resampling, expanding-window prediction, and time-alignment audits.

Data and research design

The empirical implementation uses JPMaQS via DataQuery; FX research additionally uses supplemental public macro and exchange-rate sources. The public repository contains code, interfaces, and a synthetic demonstration. Source observations, processed panels, and detailed empirical outputs are excluded.

Recorded findings

The recorded exploratory longer-history study did not reliably identify a half-life. Main financing-feedback models did not establish a robust loop under simultaneous intervals.

How to interpret the evidence

Predictive feedback does not establish a causal loop. Interpretation depends on data vintages, observation timing, sample coverage, and the family of comparisons. The README summarizes recorded exploratory findings; withheld empirical inputs and outputs are not included for independent reproduction.
