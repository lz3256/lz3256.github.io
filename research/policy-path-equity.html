Policy Path and Equity Returns

A research prototype linking policy-path repricing to equity sensitivity and portfolio construction.

Macro signalsResearch prototype
Repository ↗

Research question

How can changes in a market-implied policy path be translated into equity forecasts and risk-controlled portfolio targets?

Work completed

Implemented fixed-delivery-month signals, historical equity sensitivity estimates, recursive forecasts, multi-horizon comparisons, and portfolio accounting with turnover and cost scenarios.

Research design

The prototype separates information available at the decision time from subsequent holding-period returns. Synthetic examples exercise calendar alignment, missing data, and portfolio accounting.

Implementation status

Synthetic demonstrations and execution comparisons ran end to end. Real-market empirical validation remains unfinished.

How to interpret the evidence

Demonstration returns are generated under synthetic assumptions. Portfolio constraints and execution conventions are research assumptions rather than validated trading conditions.
