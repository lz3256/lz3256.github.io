Alpha Decay Lab

How overlapping signals, decay assumptions, and trading costs affect signal reconstruction.

Signal reconstructionMechanism experiments

Research question

When does a misspecified decay shape distort signal reconstruction, and can validation-selected shrinkage improve the resulting decisions?

Work completed

Implemented synthetic signal experiments, Wiener and Kalman reconstruction, estimated and oracle comparisons, cost ablations, and parallel runs with auditable checkpoints.

Experiment design

Synthetic data support controlled comparisons of decay shape, signal overlap, and noise. A separate quote-data adapter supports engineering checks; its data and outputs are not included here.

Implementation status

A small-scale experiment suite has been completed. The larger research configurations define further comparisons rather than completed empirical findings.

How to interpret the evidence

Synthetic experiments test mechanisms under specified assumptions. They do not demonstrate live trading performance, and the quote-data pipeline is separate from the mechanism estimator.
