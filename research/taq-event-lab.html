TAQ Event Lab

Controlled comparisons of trade-event tokenization under exposure and compute budgets.

Market microstructureTransformers
Repository ↗Data import notes ↗Compute comparison ↗Representation probes ↗

Research question

How do joint event tokens and sequential field tokens behave when the comparison budget changes?

Work completed

Implemented small Transformer baselines, cleaning audits, train-only quantization, repeated-seed comparisons, downstream direction tasks, and frozen-representation probes.

Data and design

TAQ trades for AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, and NVDA over ten trading days in February 2025. NVDA supplies the unseen-stock comparison; raw and prepared market records are excluded from the repository.

Recorded findings

Experiments show that model rankings depend on the exposure or training-time budget. Repeated seeds and downstream probes extend the likelihood comparison.

How to interpret the evidence

The sample has few dates, and some test results were inspected during development. Token likelihood and representation probes do not establish trading profitability.
